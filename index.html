Controls

Move: WASD / arrow keys, or mobile joystick

Dash: Space / mobile DASH button (5-second cooldown)

Normal shot: Hold and release left-click / F, or HOLD TO CHARGE

Curve Shot (Z): Hold and release to fire a bending shot toward the pitch centreline.

Power Shot (C): Hold until fully charged, then release to fire. Releasing early cancels the shot but still starts the 10-second cooldown.

Lob pass: Hold and release right-click / G, or HOLD TO LOB (5-second cooldown)

Charging slows your movement. Airborne balls cannot be touched or score until they land.
